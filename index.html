01 About me

A builder from Thatta who teaches, designs and automates.

I'm Sarwar Ali, a Computer Science student at the University of Sindh and an AI automation developer based in Makli, Thatta.

I build WhatsApp chatbots and AI agents with n8n, OpenAI and Supabase, ship websites through AI-assisted development (vibe coding), and analyse data with Python, SQL and Power BI. My recent work includes a multi-flow WhatsApp chatbot for Collective Pakistan that guides citizens through the Right to Information process.

Alongside building, I teach. I've trained multiple batches of students in graphic design for the Sindh government's People's IT Programme at MUET, and I'm an instructor under the NAVTTC programme. Freelancing on Fiverr since 2018 taught me to deliver for real clients.
